The version attributes have one form, ``<package>_version``: line lists and continua carry ``chianti_version``, ``chiantipy_version`` and ``fiasco_version`` in place of ``Chianti``, ``Chiantipy`` and ``fiasco``, an AIA effective area carries ``aiapy_version`` in place of ``aiapy``, and an imager response that holds a continuum carries ``continuum_chianti_version`` and ``continuum_fiasco_version``. ``reshape_x_to_slit_step`` writes ``step_size`` as one value with its unit, such as ``"0.4 arcsec"``, in place of a number and a ``step_size units`` attribute. Files written by muse 0.3 are still read: the version attributes of the line lists, continua, effective areas and responses the library opens get their new names, and ``reshape_slit_step_to_x`` reads the two former attributes.
