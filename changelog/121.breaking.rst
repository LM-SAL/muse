``read_response`` and ``load_and_concat_responses`` read only responses in the layout ``save_response`` writes: the public names, a ``line`` dimension, ``line_wavelength`` and units on the axes. The on-load renaming of the legacy MUSE names, the ``LINE_WVL``/``MAIN_LINE_WVL`` fallback and the assumed axis units are gone, and so is ``migrate_response``; legacy files are migrated once by the pipeline that owns them.
