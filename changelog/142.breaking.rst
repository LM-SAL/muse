Renamed for one name per quantity and no misleading names. Keywords: ``nslits`` and ``nraster`` are ``number_of_slits`` and ``steps_per_raster`` in ``match_fov``, ``reshape_x_to_slit_step`` and ``reshape_slit_step_to_x``; ``slit_spacing`` is ``pixels_between_slits`` in ``map_response_to_sg_detector``; ``include_gain`` is ``include_dn_per_photon`` in ``create_aia_effective_area``; ``vmax`` and ``vmask`` are ``maximum_doppler_velocity`` and ``peak_half_width`` in ``calculate_moments``. Fields of ``DEFAULTS_MUSE``: ``ccd_gain_SG`` and ``ccd_gain_CI`` are ``gain_SG`` and ``gain_CI``; ``pair_creation_energy_SG`` and ``pair_creation_energy_CI`` are ``pair_energy_SG`` and ``pair_energy_CI``. The provenance attributes that record these arguments carry the new names too.
