``create_spectral_response`` averages each line over its wavelength bin by default (``bin_method="average"``) on any grid, which conserves the flux of a line narrower than a bin; pass ``bin_method="center"`` for the previous sampling at the bin centers. ``read_response`` resamples ``doppler_velocity`` linearly by default, as ``read_responses`` and ``align_response_and_vdem`` do; pass ``doppler_velocity_method="nearest"`` for the previous behavior. ``doppler_to_wavelength`` refuses a dataset that already has a ``detector_wavelength`` coordinate, such as a mapped response, rather than replacing its detector geometry; drop the coordinate first to replace it.
