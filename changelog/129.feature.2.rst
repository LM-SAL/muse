``DEFAULTS_MUSE`` holds the abundances and the grids of the response products (``response_abundances``, ``response_temperature``, ``response_pressure``, ``response_doppler_velocity`` and ``continuum_response_doppler_velocity``), and the half width and the sampling step of the waveband of an SG channel (``waveband_half_width_SG`` and ``wavelength_step_SG``). ``threadpoolctl`` is a new dependency: the generators and the caches cap the BLAS and OpenMP threads with it, and those of numexpr when it is installed, at their ``threads`` argument.
