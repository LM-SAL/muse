``DEFAULTS_MUSE`` holds the abundances and the grids of the response products (``response_abundances``, ``response_temperature``, ``response_pressure``, ``response_doppler_velocity`` and ``continuum_response_doppler_velocity``) and the wavelengths an SG channel covers (``waveband_half_width_SG`` and ``wavelength_step_SG``). ``threadpoolctl`` is a new dependency: the generators and the caches cap the BLAS and OpenMP threads with it, and those of numexpr when it is installed, by default at 4.
