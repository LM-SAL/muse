The AIA 94 Å response example builds its temperature response with ``create_spectral_response``, ``transform_response_units`` and ``map_response_to_ci_detector`` in place of a radiometric conversion computed by hand, and the AIA 94 Å synthesis example takes its area, pixel size and camera from ``create_aia_effective_area`` and its main lines from ``rank_line_list``. Both integrate each line over its wavelength bin (``bin_method="average"``): an iron line is narrower than their bins, and sampled at the bin centers it was miscounted, so that the cool lines of the response example lost about half their signal and the synthesized image was 3.6 times too faint.
