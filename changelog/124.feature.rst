Added the response pipeline. ``create_spectral_response`` gains ``bin_method="average"`` (each bin holds the average of the line's Gaussian, so the line area is conserved on bins wider than the lines, with the effective area taken at each line's center) and ``wavelength_edges`` for any bin layout, with ``create_log_wavelength_edges`` for bins of constant velocity width; every response now carries a ``wavelength_bounds`` coordinate. ``create_fiasco_line_list`` builds the line-list layout on fiasco (``fiasco`` extra), and ``apply_line_boosts``, ``select_transitions`` and ``rank_line_list`` scale, select and order transitions. ``create_chianti_continuum``, ``create_fiasco_continuum`` and ``bin_continuum`` compute the free-free, free-bound and two-photon continuum with either backend and fold it through an effective area onto bins. ``generate_responses`` and ``generate_continuum_responses`` write the SG and CI products of every band, abundance and plasma point, with ``load_or_create_line_list`` and ``load_or_create_continuum`` caching per abundance; ``create_effective_area``, ``read_effective_area``, ``select_effective_area_curve`` and ``effective_area_wavelength_range`` define the effective-area file, and ``save_spectral_response`` and ``read_spectral_response`` the waveband cubes. ``DEFAULTS_MUSE`` gains ``waveband_half_width_SG`` and ``wavelength_step_SG``. The cluster and local drivers live in ``scripts/response_baseline``.
