Added the response pipeline: ``generate_responses`` and ``generate_continuum_responses`` write the SG and CI products of every band, abundance and plasma point from cached line lists and continua. New building blocks: ``create_fiasco_line_list``, ``apply_line_boosts``, ``select_transitions`` and ``rank_line_list``; ``create_chianti_continuum``, ``create_fiasco_continuum`` and ``bin_continuum``; ``bin_method="average"``, ``wavelength_edges`` and ``wavelength_bounds`` in ``create_spectral_response`` with ``create_log_wavelength_edges``; the effective-area file (``create_effective_area``, ``read_effective_area``, ``select_effective_area_curve``) and the waveband-cube files (``save_spectral_response``, ``read_spectral_response``). The drivers live in ``scripts/response_baseline``.
