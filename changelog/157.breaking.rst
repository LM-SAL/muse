Temperature, pressure and density grids are plain 1-D Quantities everywhere, and ``read_response`` and ``read_responses`` take their axes as Quantities only. ``InstrumentDefaults`` holds instrument properties only: the response grids, abundances, ``minimum_abundance``, normalization and the defaults of ``match_fov`` and ``vdem_synthesis`` are now the defaults of those functions. ``read_response`` and ``read_responses`` no longer take ``gain``; give it to ``transform_flux_units``. ``dx_pixel_*`` and ``dy_pixel_*`` are ``pixel_width_*`` and ``pixel_height_*``, ``detector_x_pixels_SG`` is an int and ``main_lines_by_channel_SG`` is keyed by channel. ``channel`` is keyword-only in ``transform_response_units`` and ``map_response_to_ci_detector``, the arguments after the first are keyword-only in ``create_chianti_line_list`` and ``reshape_x_to_slit_step``, and ``peak_half_width`` is in pixels.
