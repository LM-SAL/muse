Added ``create_effective_area``, ``save_effective_area`` and ``read_effective_area`` for the effective area of one channel, ``effective_area_wavelength_range`` for the range a set of them covers, and ``create_aia_effective_area`` for an SDO/AIA channel in cm2 from aiapy (``pip install "muse[aia]"``). ``create_spectral_response``, ``create_continuum_response`` and ``rank_line_list`` take such a Dataset as ``effective_area``.
