Added ``sum_lines_per_channel`` to ``muse.synthesis``, which sums the lines of each channel of a spectrum or a response, as the detector records them on the same pixels. ``channel`` replaces ``line`` as the dimension, and ``sum_slits=True`` also sums over ``slit``. A channel whose lines differ in wavelength gets a NaN ``line_wavelength``, and ``transform_flux_units`` refuses to convert an entry without a line wavelength between photons and electrons or DN: convert before summing.
