The products of ``generate_sg_responses``, ``generate_sg_continuum_responses`` and ``generate_ci_responses`` are named after what selects them, in a folder per abundance: ``<output>/<abundance>/response_sg_<channel>_<line>_<kind>_<label>_<plasma>.zarr.zip`` and ``<output>/<abundance>/response_ci_<channel>[_waveband]_<label>_<plasma>[_DN].zarr.zip``, in place of ``<output>/respfunc_..._<abundance>_<label>_<plasma>_<author>_<date>.zarr.zip``. A detector response is ``detector`` in place of ``nslits<number>_sep<separation>``, and its ``spectral_slit_separation`` attribute states the separation, such as ``390 mAngstrom``; the author and the date are attributes alone, and ``author`` may hold any character. An output directory that holds products under the former names stops the call. The ``instrument_defaults`` attribute of a product is ``DEFAULTS_MUSE`` or ``custom`` and a digest of the values, so ``skip_existing=True`` keeps no product made with other values, the MUSE values of another muse included. ``read_responses`` takes the paths of the files, in place of a directory, file names and ``channels``: each file gives its channel in its ``channel`` coordinate and its camera gain in its ``gain`` coordinate or attribute, as the products do, and files of different instrument defaults or units are refused. ``read_response`` takes the gain from the file by default, in place of the value of ``DEFAULTS_MUSE`` for its channel.
