``map_response_to_sg_detector``, ``generate_sg_responses``, ``generate_sg_continuum_responses`` and ``generate_ci_responses`` take ``instrument_defaults``, an ``InstrumentDefaults`` used in place of ``DEFAULTS_MUSE`` for every instrument value they look up: the detector geometry and calibration, the spectral orders, the wavebands, the instrument FWHM and the main lines. For example ``generate_sg_responses(..., instrument_defaults=attrs.evolve(DEFAULTS_MUSE, number_of_slits_SG=5))``. Each product records in its ``instrument_defaults`` attribute whether these were the MUSE values or others. A detector value that neither the effective-area file nor ``instrument_defaults`` gives raises rather than falling back to ``DEFAULTS_MUSE``, and ``skip_existing=True`` does not keep a product made with the other kind of defaults, nor one made before this record existed.
