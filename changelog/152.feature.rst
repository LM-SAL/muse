``read_response`` gives a response a ``pair_energy`` coordinate from the ``pair_energy`` attribute of its file, as it does the gain, and ``transform_flux_units`` takes the pair energy and the gain from the coordinates of a spectrum made from such a response before the values of ``DEFAULTS_MUSE``; ``read_responses`` refuses files of which some state their pair energy and some do not. The products of the generators carry a ``settings_digest`` of every input that shapes them, the values of their effective area included, and ``skip_existing=True`` keeps no product made with other settings: another grid, effective area or detector, main line or FWHM of its channel, line-list file, which is told by its size and the time it was written, or contaminant count; settings that do not shape a product, such as the author, the caches or the main lines of another channel, are left out. The digest of the instrument defaults changes with this, so products of earlier development versions are neither kept nor read with new ones: make them again. The responses of ``create_spectral_response`` and ``create_continuum_response``, and so the products, inherit the history of their effective area.
