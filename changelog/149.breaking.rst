The provenance attributes are ``history``, ``date_created``, ``date_modified`` and ``muse_version`` in place of ``HISTORY``, ``date created``, ``date modified`` and ``version``. Each history entry ends with the UTC time and the muse version of its call, as in ``map_response_to_sg_detector(channel=171.0 Angstrom, number_of_slits=35, ...) [2026-11-16T02:12:40Z muse 0.4.0]``, keeps the units of its inputs, and gives an array as its size and range. ``date_created`` and ``muse_version`` are written once, when the data is made, and the dates are ISO 8601 in UTC. ``read_response`` and ``read_responses`` add their entry but change neither, nor ``date_modified``, so a product read later still says when and by which muse it was made. Options that only say how the code ran (``workers``, ``threads``, ``chunked``, ``chunks``, ``create_if_missing``, ``skip_existing``, ``logT_method``, ``doppler_velocity_method``, ``device`` and ``progress``) are recorded in the entry and no longer stored as attributes.
