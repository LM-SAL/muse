Attributes are renamed: ``HISTORY``, ``date created``, ``date modified`` and ``version`` → ``history``, ``date_created``, ``date_modified`` and ``muse_version``, and the ``Chianti`` and ``Chiantipy`` attributes of a line list → ``chianti_version`` and ``chiantipy_version``. Dates are ISO 8601 times in UTC, each history entry ends with its time and muse version, and reading a file no longer changes its dates or ``muse_version``. Run options such as ``chunked`` go into the history instead of attributes.
