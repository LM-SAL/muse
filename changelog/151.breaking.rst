Line lists hold their transitions along ``transition`` instead of ``trans_index``, and must keep that dimension: select one with ``.isel(transition=[i])``, not ``.isel(transition=i)``. ``reshape_x_to_slit_step`` writes ``step_size`` with its unit, such as ``"0.4 arcsec"``. Files of muse 0.3 are not converted: use ``line_list.rename(trans_index="transition")``, and set ``raster.attrs["step_size"] = f"{raster.attrs['step_size']} {raster.attrs['step_size units']}"`` before ``reshape_slit_step_to_x``.
