``DEFAULTS_MUSE.spectral_slit_separation_SG`` holds one value per SG channel, as an `xarray.DataArray` along ``channel``, in place of one value for all: select a channel with ``.sel(channel=171)``. Each is the nominal separation of the slits at the spectral order of its channel, 390, 390 and 780 mAngstrom for 108, 171 and 284, in place of the second-order value for every channel. ``map_response_to_sg_detector`` takes the value of its channel as it is, so the responses do not change.
