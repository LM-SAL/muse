``match_fov`` takes ``pad_mode``, ``oversampling`` and ``extent`` in place of ``mode``, ``sub_interpolation`` and ``x_extent``, and ``extent="extend"`` in place of ``"tile"``; the defaults ``fov_mode``, ``fov_sub_interpolation`` and ``fov_x_extent`` are ``fov_pad_mode``, ``fov_oversampling`` and ``fov_extent``. ``rotate=True`` swapped the names of x and y, which mirrored the field; ``rotation`` takes a multiple of 90 degrees and turns the field counter-clockwise, so pass ``rotation=90 * u.deg`` for a quarter turn. ``calculate_moments`` takes ``variable_name`` in place of ``integration_name``. ``map_response_to_sg_detector`` takes ``detector_x_pixels`` in place of ``detector_pixels``, and the defaults ``pixels_SG``, ``initial_wavelength_SG`` and ``main_lines_SG`` are ``detector_x_pixels_SG``, ``wavelength_start_SG`` and ``main_lines_by_channel_SG``. ``read_response`` takes ``number_of_slits``, an integer, in place of ``slit``, an array whose maximum gave the count; ``load_and_concat_responses`` is ``read_responses`` and takes it too.
