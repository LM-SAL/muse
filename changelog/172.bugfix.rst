`muse.synthesis.create_simple_vdem` raises a ``ValueError`` naming the grid when ``velocity_grid`` or ``logT_grid`` is not increasing and evenly spaced, or has a single value. It had binned the whole grid with its first spacing, so on a ``velocity_grid`` of ``[0, 10, 40]`` km/s a 30 km/s flow towards the observer landed in the 10 km/s bin; grids made with ``arange`` or ``linspace``, including in float32, are still accepted.
