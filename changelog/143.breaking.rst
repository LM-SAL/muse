Physical inputs that were bare numbers take astropy Quantities, so a value in another unit is converted instead of being read wrongly: the cubes, axes and grids of ``create_simple_vdem``, whose arguments are now keyword-only and whose ``velocity_axis`` and ``log_temperature_axis`` are ``velocity_grid`` and ``logT_grid`` (in ``dex(K)``); ``maximum_doppler_velocity`` of ``calculate_moments``; and ``channels`` of ``load_and_concat_responses``, e.g. ``[108, 171, 284] * u.AA``. ``read_response`` and ``load_and_concat_responses`` take ``logT`` and ``doppler_velocity`` as a Quantity or as a DataArray with units, and refuse one without units instead of reading it in the units of the file.
