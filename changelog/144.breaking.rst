``create_spectral_response`` takes the instrument width as a FWHM, ``instrumental_fwhm``, in place of a Gaussian sigma under ``instrumental_width``, and ``generate_sg_responses`` takes ``instrumental_fwhm_by_channel`` in place of ``instrumental_width_by_channel``. ``DEFAULTS_MUSE.instrumental_fwhm_SG`` holds the FWHM of each SG channel, and the property ``instrumental_width_SG`` is removed. Pass ``DEFAULTS_MUSE.instrumental_fwhm_SG.sel(channel=171)`` where ``DEFAULTS_MUSE.instrumental_width_SG.sel(channel=171)`` was passed.
