``create_spectral_response`` takes the instrument width as a FWHM, ``instrumental_fwhm``, instead of the Gaussian sigma ``instrumental_width``: multiply a sigma by 2.3548. ``DEFAULTS_MUSE.instrumental_fwhm_SG`` holds the FWHM of each SG channel at its spectral order (0.04075, 0.04075 and 0.0815 Å for 108, 171 and 284) instead of the first-order 0.0815 Å, and ``instrumental_width_SG`` is removed: pass ``instrumental_fwhm=DEFAULTS_MUSE.instrumental_fwhm_SG.sel(channel=171).data``.
