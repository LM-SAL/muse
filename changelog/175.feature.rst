``muse.synthesis.create_simple_vdem`` takes ``temperature_bin_method``: ``"span"``, the default, bins as before, and ``"linear"`` shares each cell's emission between the two log T bin centers nearest its own log T, as it does its velocity, so the VDEM keeps each cell's mean log T. The message giving the share of emission hotter or faster than the grids is now a warning instead of info.
