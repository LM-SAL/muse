These arguments need astropy Quantities, such as ``200 * u.km / u.s``: the arrays of ``create_simple_vdem``, with ``logT_grid`` in dex(K), such as ``np.arange(5.5, 7.6, 0.1) * u.dex(u.K)``; ``maximum_doppler_velocity`` (km/s) and ``peak_half_width`` (pixels) of ``calculate_moments``; ``logT`` (dex(K)) and ``doppler_velocity`` of ``read_response`` and ``read_responses``, which refuse a DataArray (pass ``vdem.logT.values * u.dex(u.K)``); and ``temperature``, ``density`` and ``pressure`` of ``create_chianti_line_list`` (pass a former DataArray's ``.data``). Arguments after the first are keyword-only in ``create_chianti_line_list`` and ``reshape_x_to_slit_step``, all of them in ``create_simple_vdem``, and ``channel`` in ``map_response_to_ci_detector`` and ``transform_response_units``.
