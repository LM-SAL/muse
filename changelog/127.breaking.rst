``map_response_to_ci_detector`` sums the wavelength bins of a response that carries ``wavelength_bounds``, as every response from ``create_spectral_response`` now does, instead of integrating between the bin centers, so imager responses change slightly at the two ends of the grid. A response without bounds is integrated as before. ``create_spectral_response`` refuses a ``wavelength_grid`` of one sample, whose bin has no width.
